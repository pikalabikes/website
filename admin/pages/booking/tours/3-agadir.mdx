Join us for a unique E-bike cultural cycling tour that takes you beyond the typical tourist routes and into the real heart of Agadir.
Led by our passionate Tour hosts, this journey blends history, tradition, and modern coastal life, giving you an authentic experience of the city.

__What You’ll Experience__

* __Mosque__: A key landmark of Agadir, offering insight into the city’s spiritual life and modern identity.

* __Town Hall__: Discover the administrative heart of the city and learn about Agadir’s rebuilding and development.

* __Olhão Garden__: A peaceful green space inspired by Agadir’s connection with Portugal, perfect for a calm nature break.

* __Traditional Oven__: Step into a local bakery and see traditional Moroccan bread being prepared in a clay oven.

* __Hammam__: Explore the traditional Moroccan bathhouse and its rituals, history, and social role in the community.

* __House of Artisanat__: Meet local artisans and discover Moroccan craftsmanship and handmade traditions.

* __Cinema Salam (Pass by)__: Pass by a historic cinema that reflects Agadir’s cultural and urban evolution.

* __Souk El Had (Pass by)__: Pass by one of the city’s most vibrant markets filled with colors, spices, and local energy.

* __Corniche__: End the ride along Agadir’s coastline, enjoying ocean views and a relaxed seaside atmosphere.

__Tour Highlight__:

* Moroccan culture and traditions

* Moroccan tea and traditional bakery experience

* Moroccan craftsmanship and architecture

* Authentic market life

* Beautiful coastal scenery

* Relaxed cycling pace through the city
