Join us for a unique cultural cycling tour that takes you beyond the typical tourist routes and into the real heart of Marrakech. Led by our passionate local host, this journey blends history, tradition, and modern life, giving you an authentic experience of the Red City.

__What You’ll Experience__

* __Traditional Moroccan Bakery__ : Step inside a local bakery, watch bread being baked in a clay oven, and taste fresh Moroccan bread while learning about its role in daily life.

* __Moroccan Tile Workshop__ : Meet artisans and see firsthand how the world-famous zellige tiles are made, a tradition that has decorated Morocco’s palaces and mosques for centuries.

* __Royal Palace Gardens__ : Pass by the beautiful public gardens surrounding the Royal Palace (Dar El-Makhzen) and admire its impressive architecture from the outside while learning about its importance in Moroccan monarchy and history.

* __Traditional Hammam__ : Visit a Moroccan public bathhouse and uncover the rituals, traditions, and social role of the hammam in daily Moroccan life.

* __Modern Marrakech (Gueliz & Hivernage)__: Cycle through wide boulevards, trendy cafes, and luxury hotels to see how Marrakech balances tradition and modernity.

__Tour Highlight__:

* Traditional bread-making and the cultural importance of bread.

* The art and history of Moroccan zellige tiles.

* Admiring the Royal Palace from its public gardens.

* Cultural immersion in the hammam tradition.

* A ride through modern Marrakech for a contrast with the old medina.

__It’s a journey through Marrakech’s history, traditions, and vibrant daily life.__
