Leave the busy streets of Marrakech behind and enjoy a peaceful cycling adventure through the palm groves and villages of the Palmeraie. This tour blends nature, tradition, and local culture, giving you a refreshing experience just outside the city.

**What You’ll Experience:**

* **Villas and Villages of the Palmeraie** : Cycle past impressive villas and palaces, then see the contrast with the humble and authentic lifestyle of the local villagers.

* **The Heart of the Palmeraie** : Discover the ancient irrigation system that carries water from the Atlas Mountains and sustains the palm trees, gardens, and farms of this oasis.

* **Traditional Village Breakfast** : Pause for a traditional Moroccan breakfast with fresh bread, olives, and mint tea, while enjoying the quiet rhythms of village life.

* **Village Oven** :Visit a communal oven where villagers bake their bread daily and learn about Morocco’s strong bread-making traditions.

* **Moroccan Tile Workshop** : Meet artisans and watch how the colorful zellige tiles are crafted by hand, a centuries-old art form central to Moroccan architecture and design.

**Tour Highlights:**

* Explore the contrast between luxurious villas and traditional village life.
* Learn about Morocco’s ingenious irrigation systems.
* Share a fresh Moroccan breakfast in a peaceful setting.
* Experience traditional bread baking in a village oven.
* Discover the artistry of Moroccan zellige tiles.
