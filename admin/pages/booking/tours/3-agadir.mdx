Join us for a unique E-bike cultural cycling tour that takes you beyond the typical tourist routes and into the real heart of Agadir. Led by our passionate Tour hosts, this journey blends history, tradition, and modern coastal life, giving you an authentic experience of the city.

What You’ll Experience

Mosque: A key landmark of Agadir, offering insight into the city’s spiritual life and modern identity.
Town Hall: Discover the administrative heart of the city and learn about Agadir’s rebuilding and development.
Olhão Garden: A peaceful green space inspired by Agadir’s connection with Portugal, perfect for a calm nature break.
Traditional Oven: Step into a local bakery and see traditional Moroccan bread being prepared in a clay oven.
Hammam: Explore the traditional Moroccan bathhouse and its rituals, history, and social role in the community.
House of Artisanat: Meet local artisans and discover Moroccan craftsmanship and handmade traditions.
Cinema Salam (Pass by): Pass by a historic cinema that reflects Agadir’s cultural and urban evolution.
Souk El Had (Pass by): Pass by one of the city’s most vibrant markets filled with colors, spices, and local energy.
Corniche: End the ride along Agadir’s coastline, enjoying ocean views and a relaxed seaside atmosphere.

Tour Highlights:

Moroccan culture and traditions
Moroccan tea and traditional bakery experience
Moroccan craftsmanship and architecture
Authentic market life

Relaxed cycling pace through the city
